# Contributor Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of the Davarus Bell Platform, we pledge to foster an open, welcoming, and professional environment.

## Our Standards

* Demonstrating empathy, respect, and professionalism.
* Focusing on what is best for the team, the platform, and our clients.
* Respecting differing viewpoints and constructive feedback.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or unethically unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting `support@rjbusinesssolutions.org`.
